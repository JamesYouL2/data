Dynasty buy / sell

Paste a Sleeper league ID, pick your team, and get a buy, hold, sell or cut call for every player, depending on whether you're rebuilding or contending. The calls come from backtests of FantasyPros dynasty rankings against what players went on to score.

The league ID is the long number in the league's URL on sleeper.com (or in the app under League settings). Enter a username to choose from that user's leagues.

What the calls mean
Buy
Historically returned more than its price. Trade for it.
Buy with a 3rd / 4th
Priced around a 3rd- or 4th-round rookie pick (ranks 151-239 and 240-300) and returned more than that pick did.
Hold
Price and production roughly agree. Keep it unless an offer beats the price.
Hold to February
Rookies whose price tends to rise through the offseason.
Use, then sell
A contender's starter whose long-term value is fading: play him through your playoffs, then sell.
Sell / Sell high now
Historically returned less than its price, or the price is about to fall. Move it while it's worth something.
Sell for a 3rd / 4th
A 3rd- or 4th-round pick returned more than players like this.
Lottery ticket
Cheap, mostly returns nothing, occasionally hits. Keep if you have the roster spot.
Cut
Players like this returned close to nothing. Free the roster spot.
